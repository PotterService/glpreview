Card care tips

1. Sleeve it right awayA penny or perfect-fit sleeve keeps scratches and fingerprints off the moment a card comes out of the pack.
2. Top loader the big hitsSleeved chase cards slide into a top loader or semi-rigid holder so they can’t bend.
3. Store them standing upDeck boxes and storage boxes keep cards upright, away from sunlight, heat and damp.
4. Side-loading bindersSide-loading pages keep cards from sliding out. Skip rubber bands and ring binders that can dent edges.
